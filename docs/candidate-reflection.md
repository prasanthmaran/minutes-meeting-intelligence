# Candidate reflection — write this yourself

These are prompts, not completed personal statements. The assessment explicitly asks for your own thinking.

- Why did you choose Meeting Intelligence over the other three options?
- Which implementation decisions did you accept, change, or reject? Give one concrete example.
- Why is the chosen scope appropriate for your actual deadline?
- Explain the chunk-size/overlap trade-off using a real passage from the sample.
- What does a valid citation prove? What does it fail to prove?
- Which AI-generated change needed correction? What check caught it?
- What did you personally test, and what evidence did you observe?
- How would your design change for multiple customers or sensitive meeting data?
- What is the highest-priority next improvement, and why?
- What are your do's and don'ts for AI coding tools? Examples: never paste secrets; review dependency additions; ask for tests around failure modes; verify generated claims; keep changes bounded.
